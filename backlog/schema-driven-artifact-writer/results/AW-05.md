## 1. Executive Summary

Card AW-05 implemented typed patch application and Core-owned immutable field protection in `src/deltafuse/core/artifact_patch.py`. It executes JSON Pointer `set` and `remove` operations, enforces Core-owned field protection (rejecting mutations to `/status`, `/id`, `/change`, `/framework`, `/schema_version`), rejects numeric array index edits (requiring whole-value array replacements in v1), rejects ancestor/descendant pointer overlaps, preserves untouched fields and body bytes, and verifies post-patch candidate validity against the storage schema.
