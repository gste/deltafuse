## 1. Executive Summary

Card AW-04 implemented the deterministic YAML and frontmatter serializer codec in `src/deltafuse/core/artifact_codec.py`. It enforces standard key ordering per artifact kind, strict scalar double-quoting for reserved strings (`yes`, `no`, `true`, `false`, `null`, `2026-09-18`, leading-zero digits), literal block scalar formatting (`|`) for multiline strings, pre-serialization round-trip equality validation (`strict_parse_yaml`), body byte preservation, and explicit `canonicalize_metadata=True` opt-in requirements with preview hashes when updating non-canonical frontmatter.
