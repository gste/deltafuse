## 1. Executive Summary

Card AW-13 integrated Core-owned status transitions (`advance_change`, `set_artifact_status`) and Human Gate decisions (`apply_decision`) beneath `ProductMutationLock` concurrency protection across `src/deltafuse/core/transitions.py`, `src/deltafuse/core/decide.py`, `tests/integration/test_core_transitions.py`, `tests/unit/test_decide.py`, and `tests/integration/test_artifact_transitions.py`.

It enforces:
1. Shared Mutation Locking: All status transitions (`advance_change`, `set_artifact_status`) and human decisions (`apply_decision`) acquire `ProductMutationLock(product_root)` during state validation, frontmatter updates, and receipt journaling.
2. Crash Recovery & Journal Invariants: Retains fail-safe receipt chain replay (`receipt_chain_errors`), receipt-backed crash recovery (`resume_incomplete`), and protection against dual uncoordinated journals.
3. Protected Core Status Rights: Denies Worker attempts to bypass status authority via generic artifact payloads or nested patches (`core_owned_field`).
4. Idempotency & Re-application Protection: Re-applying `apply_decision` on an already accepted/rejected decision or spec-delta fails cleanly (`DecideError`) without polluting receipts or journal files.

## 4. Platform Limitations & Scope Note

Core mutation lock uses `ProductMutationLock` (`msvcrt` on NT, `fcntl` on POSIX). Non-cooperating external processes opening files without lock acquisition bypass OS lock semantics.
