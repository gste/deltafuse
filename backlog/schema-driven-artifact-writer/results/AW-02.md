## 1. Executive Summary

Card AW-02 implemented the strict bounded artifact and YAML reader in `src/deltafuse/core/artifact_reader.py`. It rejects duplicate YAML/JSON keys, non-finite floats (`.nan`, `.inf`), custom tags, and oversized payloads (>1MB). It splits frontmatter delimiters (`---`) without modifying or stripping markdown body bytes, preserving trailing blank lines, CRLF line endings, and non-ASCII UTF-8 characters byte-for-byte.
