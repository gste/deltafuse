   pyproject package-data glob already ships the new YAML; no packaging setting

   damaged/old bundle cannot fall back to a caller-controlled cwd contract.
   Without a bundle, source lookup is anchored to the installed module's checkout,
   using `docs/contracts`, not the nonexistent `process/contracts` location.

   repair hint. The English/Russian canonical contract documents this diagnostic.
   No envelope fields, semantic defaults, schema enums or authorization rules
   changed; skills and storage schemas require no corresponding behavior change.

   `Locker_PATH` declaration typo in `scripts/init.sh` (all uses are `LOCK_PATH`).
   Scope was expanded by this one-line installer repair. Its authentic Red is
   retained, followed by a successful fresh-install/upgrade smoke. No other
