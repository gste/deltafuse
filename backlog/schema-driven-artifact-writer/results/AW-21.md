## 1. Summary of Changes

Remediated Review Finding 1 where Worker-authorized updates could write to absolute targets outside their Change, missing authorization contexts skipped policy checks, halted stages were not checked, and commit lock revalidation compared a context against itself.

### Key Modifications:
- `src/deltafuse/core/artifact_policy.py`:
  - Updated `validate_artifact_policy` to reject null `auth_ctx`, halted/inactive stages (`halted`, `accepted`, `converged`, `archived`), targets outside `product_root`, and targets outside the owning `change_id` boundary (`docs/changes/<change_id>/`).
- `src/deltafuse/core/artifacts.py`:
  - Updated `_resolve_target_path` to enforce `product_root` containment and path traversal protection for absolute and relative paths before returning.
  - Made `validate_artifact_policy` check mandatory on all `create` and `update` calls (raises `null_authorization_context` when `auth_context` is missing).
  - Made `TransactionManager` instantiation lazy on `ArtifactService` so read-only operations (`validate`, `describe`) do not create internal `.deltafuse/journal` or `.deltafuse/receipts` directories.
- `src/deltafuse/core/artifact_lock.py`:
  - Updated `revalidate_authority` to reject null context, inactive/halted stages, and fingerprint/attribute mismatches.
- `src/deltafuse/core/artifact_transactions.py`:
  - Deferred `.deltafuse/journal` and `.deltafuse/receipts` directory creation to active transaction preparation (`prepare_transaction`).
- `tests/integration/test_artifact_security.py`:
  - Added `test_aw21_reproduce_security_failures` covering outside-Change writes, missing-context writes, and halted stage envelope writes.
- `tests/unit/test_artifact_service.py`:
  - Updated `test_validate_is_strictly_read_only` with strict assertions that no internal journal or receipt directories are created.
