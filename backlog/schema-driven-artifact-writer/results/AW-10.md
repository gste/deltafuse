## 1. Executive Summary

Card AW-10 implemented `src/deltafuse/core/artifacts.py` (`ArtifactService`) and unit test suite `tests/unit/test_artifact_service.py`. It wires `create`, `update`, `validate`, and `describe` operations through one unified service interface with full transaction sequencing, bounded storage schema validation, Core-owned field protection (`status`, `id`, `change`, `schema_version`, `framework`), atomic same-filesystem publication, and read-only `validate` and `describe` guarantees (ensuring zero side-effects on journal or target files).
