## Changes and scope

The closed run schema binds campaign/run/case/variant/session/sandbox and
profile/contract/registry/host identities. It represents raw score, exact
published ceiling kinds and limits, final score, release verdict, seven stage
references, system/attestation references, hard failures, and the complete
failure taxonomy. Passed and failed runs require numeric scores in 1..10000;
invalid runs require null raw/final scores and an invalid verdict.

The closed campaign schema binds fixed profile, contract, registry, host,
generator, and membership identities. It requires at least three explicit
members with independent run/session/sandbox/schedule identities. Executed
members require report references; missing and not-run members require null
report/score fields; invalid members preserve their report but cannot carry a
numeric score. Aggregate diagnostics remain present when campaign score is
null.
