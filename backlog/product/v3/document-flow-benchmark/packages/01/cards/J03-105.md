## Changes and scope

The closed attestation schema binds DeltaFuse commit/version/wheel/lock,
executor inspect/dependency evidence, exact agent package/launcher/dependency/
redacted-config/extension identities, model/provider/weights/quantization/
tokenizer/endpoint identity, host/runtime/dependency/resource/network hashes,
and digest/platform/inspect evidence for every image.

Measured attestations alone may set `release_eligible=true` and require actual
positive context-window and maximum-output measurements. Declared-only records
remain representable but must be non-release. Configuration content has no
storage field: only its redacted hash, redaction field names, manifest hash,
and evidence reference are allowed; `contains_secret_values` is fixed false.

The closed variant schema binds a non-boolean seed, full generator source
revision and hashes, operation-stream hash/count/evidence, and explicit
ambiguity/workload IDs.
