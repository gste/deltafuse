## Changes and scope

The closed stage schema requires report/predecessor identity, full framework,
agent/model and endpoint identity, lifecycle state, immutable visits and
snapshots, per-task attempts, attestation/event references, deterministic
checks, measurement counters/provenance, bounded component totals, hard
failures, and test-result evidence. Not-reached stages must have no visits,
attempts, snapshots, events, checks, or test results and carry zero stage totals.

The closed system schema represents passed, failed, invalid, and not-reached
outcomes. Valid product failures retain numeric diagnostic totals and evidence;
infrastructure/host-model/oracle/provenance invalidity requires a null total and
cannot be labelled as a product failure. Passed and failed executions require
snapshot, event, measurement-provenance, scenario, and test-result evidence.
