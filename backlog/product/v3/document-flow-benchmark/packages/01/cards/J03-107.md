records. It validates at least three members; unique run/session/sandbox IDs;
fixed profile, host, variant, seed and schedule identity; exact required-member
coverage; and semantic-result hashes. It recomputes each run's stage/system/raw/
ceiling/final consistency and rejects claimed campaign totals.

an incomplete campaign, invalid runs yield an invalid campaign, and both carry
a null campaign score while preserving available scores and diagnostics. Valid
campaigns use exact rational median/min/mean weighting with half-even rounding,
population variance, pass rate over every required member, deterministic first
failure counts, and per-stage ranges/means.

variant incompatibility or null campaign scores. Compatible completed campaigns
return score and exact per-stage mean deltas.
