registered check, exact per-stage factors with provenance, and all four frozen
absolute gates. It rejects missing/duplicate/unknown facts, prerequisite
contradictions, incomplete gate/stage sets, malformed evidence, floats,
booleans-as-numbers, out-of-range or oversized rationals, unknown failure
classes, and any caller-supplied totals.

Efficiency uses exact `Fraction` arithmetic and nearest-integer ties-to-even.

1..10000, records every applicable 4999/6999/1999 ceiling, and applies the
minimum. Build/functional failure implies the functional ceiling. Invalidity
precedes numeric scoring and produces null raw/final scores.
