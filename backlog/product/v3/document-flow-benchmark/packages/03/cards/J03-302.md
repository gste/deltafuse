## Changes and scope

`oracle/interpreter.py` is the private pure domain oracle, hand-written from
`docs/spec/contracts/` and the target intake requirements (REQ-SUP/ROUTE/
DEL/OBS) with zero dependency on the Java services or the variant
generator: the module imports nothing but `dataclasses`/`typing`, and a
source scan test rejects product/generator dependencies by name.
``reduce(operations, stop_before=N)`` folds a full stream or a prefix
(expected-state-at-barrier for later crash-barrier checks) into an
``ExpectedState``: per-document versions and active version, per-version
routes (PENDING/APPROVED/REJECTED/SUPERSEDED, filled expert slots, decision
history), per-document audit sequences, and a canonical observation
projection (document, active_version, workflow, open_slots in
legal→security order, audit_sequence).

Independent semantics implemented and fixture-pinned: decision identity
binding (document + version + route; wrong version → `IDENTITY_MISMATCH`);
`decision_id` idempotency within a route (same canonical payload replays
silently, different payload → `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`); superseded routes
ignore late decisions with one `IGNORED_LATE_DECISION` audit record each;
closed routes reject further decisions; registrar rejection is
`UNSUPPORTED_ACTION`; a registrar decision before both expert approvals is
`EXPERT_REVIEW_INCOMPLETE`; one actor cannot fill two expert roles of one
route (`ACTOR_ROLE_MISMATCH`) while the same actor may hold the same role
on a successor route; a filled slot refuses a second decision; any expert
rejection closes the route `REJECTED`; submission of a new version
atomically supersedes the pending route and opens the successor; audit
entries are per-document sequential and ordered. Delivery, duplicate-
delivery and crash operations carry transport identity only and provably
change nothing. Out-of-contract input (unknown kinds, decisions for routes
that never existed, double document creation) raises `InterpreterError`.

The fixture set is hand-worked: normal approval with ordered audit,
expert rejection, early registrar, registrar rejection, actor reuse, wrong
version, supersede with two late decisions, silent replay, idempotency
conflict, filled slot, interleaved independent documents, transport-noise
immutability, barrier prefix equality, open-slot ordering, and
out-of-contract rejection.
