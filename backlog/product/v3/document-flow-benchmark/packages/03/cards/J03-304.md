## Changes and scope

The private `parallel-approval` patch composes after J03-303's supersede
overlay. It replaces the baseline single approver with legal and security
expert slots followed by registrar approval, while retaining route/document/
version binding, transactional inbox/state/outbox persistence, supersede, and
decision-id replay semantics. One expert leaves the route pending; both expert
approvals are required before registrar approval closes it. An expert rejection
closes the route, the same actor cannot fill both expert roles, a filled role
cannot be filled again, and registrar rejection is retained as invalid evidence.

The Flyway extension expands only the private reference vocabulary and adds a
database uniqueness constraint for one decision per route role. Invalid
decisions create one sequenced receipt/outbox item and do not mutate route or
decision state. Same-payload replays return the stored result without another
effect; different-payload reuse remains an idempotency conflict.
