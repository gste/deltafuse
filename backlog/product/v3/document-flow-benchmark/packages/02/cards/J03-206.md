## Changes and scope

`OutboxPublisher` seeds delivery receipts, locks at most the configured batch
with `FOR UPDATE ... SKIP LOCKED`, publishes with broker acknowledgement, and
marks sent in the same local PostgreSQL transaction. Failure after publish and
before the sent marker rolls the marker back, so a restarted publisher emits a
safe duplicate rather than losing the event. Scheduled adapters are disabled
unless `j03.messaging.enabled=true`; they use bounded batches and idempotent
Kafka producers.

`WorkflowEventConsumer` strictly decodes the public baseline event, invokes the
existing atomic inbox/domain/outbox command, reaches the controlled
`AFTER_COMMIT_BEFORE_ACK` hook, and only then acknowledges Kafka. Its closed DLQ
reason vocabulary is `MALFORMED_JSON`, `INVALID_CONTRACT`, and
`UNSUPPORTED_EVENT`; rejected data is represented by a bounded reason/detail
and SHA-256 rather than being interpreted or applied to domain state. Spring
wiring uses manual-immediate acknowledgement.

Forward-only V3 delivery-receipt migrations and seed POM/profile edits are a
recorded necessary scope adjustment: the card's proposed
`messaging-integration` profile and document Kafka dependencies did not exist.
